Before you start: make sure your Notion integration is connected to your database. In Notion open your database → ··· menu → Connections → select your integration.
Get yours at notion.so/my-integrations → New integration → copy the token.
Open your database in Notion → click ··· → Copy link. The ID is the last part of the URL before any ?.
Template: Your sheet must have these column headers in row 1 (in order):

Guest Name · Source · Invitation Date · Briefing Date · Interview Date · Notes · Offer Made · Assets Created · Assets Shared · Published to YouTube · Reels 1 Published · Reels 2 Published · Reels 3 Published

Use TRUE / FALSE for checkboxes and YYYY-MM-DD for dates. Then publish: File → Share → Publish to web → CSV.
Paste the regular sheet URL, the published CSV link, or just the spreadsheet ID. The sheet must be either published to the web, or publicly readable.
